A sketch of the graph for the marketplace in the Almedia track. Boxes are metrics or context events; lines show how they relate. Press "Run investigation" to see how the system follows the graph to a cause. Metrics and numbers here are examples, not real data.

Demo: how a fault gets found

    Click any box to see what it is.

    formula

    Exact arithmetic, such as orders = checkouts × payment_success_rate. Used to split a change into each part's contribution and find the biggest one.

    breakdown

    The same metric cut by country, platform or category. Used to drill down and see which segment holds the change.

    may_affect

    No formula, but a possible influence, such as stockouts lowering add-to-cart. Treated as a hypothesis, then checked with timing and correlation.

    context

    Release log, promo calendar, data arrival. Used to tell real faults from harmless changes and from data delays.
